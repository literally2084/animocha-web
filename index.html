CREATOR REVENUE

Four ways to release your work

Enable the access models that make sense for each title. Actual transactions and entitlements must be validated by the backend.

01

Free to watch

Offer high-standard streaming at no cost. Choose whether your title includes ads, based on your release strategy.

  • Ad-supported free viewing
  • Free viewing without ads
  • Creator-controlled availability
02

Pay per view

Charge for a single viewing entitlement, with clear price and access terms before checkout.

  • Single-title access
  • Backend-verified payment
  • Clear viewing window
03

Timed rental

Let viewers rent an animation for a defined window, such as 48 hours after first playback.

  • Configurable rental duration
  • Expiry enforced server-side
  • Transparent renewal terms
04

Own a copy

Sell a permanent purchase entitlement to a downloadable copy when the creator allows it.

  • Downloadable file or package
  • Clear license and usage rights
  • Ownership record in account
